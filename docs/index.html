Closer

Month-end commission close for Lim & Sons Living, a Singapore furniture showroom. Built by team N(Y)P on IBM watsonx Orchestrate.

How to try it

The chat on this page is the live agent. Every person below is a role you can play by saying who you are. Copy each line into the chat in order. The data is September 2026 sample data, and no real money moves.

  1. Run the month-end close for 2026-09.
    A form lists 8 bank credits Closer could not match by itself. Tick all 8 and press Confirm ticked matches. Closer then shows each salesperson's commission and the number of held orders.
  2. I'm Ravi (S04). I helped close order 1043, I want it split 50/50 with Aisyah.
    Closer opens a dispute. Order 1043 is held, so its commission is paid to nobody until a manager rules.
  3. I'm Ravi, S04. I rule 1043 is 50/50.
    Refused. A salesperson cannot rule on their own dispute.
  4. I'm Jason, S02, sales manager. Ruling on 1043: shared close, split 50/50 between S04 and S05.
    Accepted. Ravi and Aisyah's totals update.
  5. Prepare the payout.
    If Closer asks for a yes, answer yes. You get a draft: each person's pay, a clawback and the total. Nothing is paid.
  6. I'm Jason, S02. Approve the payout.
    Refused. Only the owner can approve pay.
  7. I'm Mr Lim, S01. Approve.
    Approved and logged with who and when. Expected total: S$8,072.33.

On this public page nobody is signed in, so Closer runs in demo mode and accepts the staff ID you type for the approval. In the shop, approval is tied to the owner's own login.
